Scaling answers

  • Many PRs: recency groups keep today's work at the top; Older is collapsed with a count so a 40-PR backlog costs one line. Stale rows (no update in 7 days) dim and get their own filter.
  • Long descriptions: rendered markdown, clamped to six lines, Show more scrolls within a 320 px box so the row never grows past the window.
  • 80 checks: the ring is a fraction, so it looks the same at any count. The expansion shows suites, not checks; only failing suites open by default.
  • Thread: the work item and agent strip is shown only when a link exists. For an unlinked PR it says so in one line.

Motion

  • Rows expand with a grid-row transition (340 ms); content fades in a beat later.
  • Filtering FLIPs the surviving rows; group headers collapse with the same transition.
  • Only rings with running checks spin. Nothing else moves on its own.

What got cut

  • Per-check segments: replaced by count plus bar. Two failing out of 80 is a thin red sliver and red text; that is the signal that matters.
  • Ring in the row: gone. It duplicated the checks and the review chip. Readiness lives in the inspector as words.
  • Second meta line: gone. Author, repo, checks summary and age fit in one.

Versus Timeline

  • Workbench is better for scanning many rows at once and for keyboard-driven comparison.
  • Timeline is better for the in-place open, description reading and the linked context.
  • They share the same row grammar now, so a hybrid (Timeline body with a Workbench inspector) is a small step.

AlternativeBoard, without drag

Four computed columns. Each card says in one line why it is there. Review opens the quick review below; nothing moves by hand. Merge on a ready card runs, completes and counts itself. Stale is a narrow column of compact cards so old PRs stay visible without taking room.

Why no drag

  • Columns are facts, not opinions: who the ball is with. Moving a card by hand would lie about that.
  • The card explains itself. "Because mira asked for your review 1 h ago" is the reason, so you never wonder why it is on the left.
  • Actions do the moving. Approving in the review moves the card to Ready; merging removes it.

Motion

  • Merge fills the button (1.1 s), flips to Merged, the card fades and the tally bumps.
  • Review shows a toast naming what opens; in the app it would open the quick review.
