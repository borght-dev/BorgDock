BorgDock · design reference · 4.17.0

Five sections, and what the rail's foot shows

The rail went from eight sections to five. Workstreams is gone. Activity and Worktrees left the rail: Activity became Recent behind a bell and a history window, Worktrees stays as the Ctrl+F7 palette. The foot of the rail shows what is left of each AI account, each machine's disk, and what needs you.

Seven artboards, lettered; Decided and Built at the end. Every name and number is made up.

Decided

  • Workstreams is deleted, not migrated. Its files stay on disk untouched; a goal is a note (D), and nothing new was built for that.
  • No telemetry. It was in the first draft and dropped before the build.
  • Activity is not a section. The bell counts what needs you now, Recent is its card, All history is a window of its own.
  • Worktrees is not a section. It stays as the Ctrl+F7 window; disk health moved to the rail, the launch sheet and Focus.
  • An account's label follows T3 Code's name for it, so the same two letters mean the same account in both apps.
  • Settings is the rail's bottom left corner. Refresh stays in the title bar.
  • Nothing is read from an AI account before the first-start sheet is answered, and never from an account the rail hides.

Built, and where the app differs

  • A to C and E to G are built as drawn. Stories: Main Window/MainWindow (RailLimitsOpen, RailMachinesOpen, RailRecentOpen), AI usage/Limits, Layout/Rail/Machines, Activity/Recent.
  • B2 shows no number of GiB a worktree would take. Where T3 puts a worktree is T3's setting, so the sheet warns on the host's tightest disk.
  • B1 offers Prune on this machine only. Another host shows how many worktrees have a merged or closed pull request.
  • A low disk notifies once, and again when it gets worse. The six-hourly reminder is gone.
  • The AI usage card's footer has Manage usage and a usage page link per provider.
  • Not drawn: under a launch sheet's model picker, a line names the account that model spends and what is left of it. This machine's T3 only.
  • The worktree changes pane went with the section. Nothing in the app shows a worktree's diff any more.
  • The iPhone app's Workstreams screens are unchanged.
