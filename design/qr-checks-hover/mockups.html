How it behaves

  • Same card, no fork. The chip wraps ChecksHoverCard in HoverPopover variant="card" with the PR list's size (420 × 640 max). Rows, reasons, shared-failure line and buttons are exactly what the check bar shows today.
  • Every state gets it: failing, running and all green. "No checks" / "CI unavailable" stay plain chips (nothing to list).
  • Placement: anchored to the chip, opening down over the diff and kept inside the window. Stays open while the pointer is on chip or card, so Rerun and Fix work; Esc closes.
  • Click: in the walk the chip becomes a button that switches the right panel to Checks. On the card (no panel) it opens the walk on Checks.
  • Only mounts while open, so the CI failure store is read only for PRs you hover, as in the list.
